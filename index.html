About

I am a PhD student and Research Assistant in Financial Economics at the University of Liechtenstein. I study how financial markets react to political and macroeconomic shocks, using tools from empirical asset pricing, derivatives, and financial econometrics.

Before my PhD I completed an MSc in Economics at the University of Freiburg. My work relies on R and Python and on reproducible empirical pipelines.

Research

Research interests

  • Empirical asset pricing
  • Political uncertainty, populism, and financial markets
  • Derivatives, option pricing, and volatility modelling
  • Financial econometrics and reproducible empirical methods
